About the artist

The evolution of Apashe

Apashe is a Belgian-born electronic music producer based in Canada. His music is recognized for combining orchestral composition with bass-heavy electronic genres, creating a distinctive dramatic style.

EARLY CAREER

Finding a distinct voice

Early releases, including “Battle Royale,” helped establish his bold, energetic approach to electronic music.

2020 · RENAISSANCE

A cinematic turning point

The album Renaissance showcased the blend of classical orchestral influences and electronic production that many listeners associate with his sound.

LATER WORK

The orchestra takes center stage

Apashe continued exploring large-scale orchestral ideas, dramatic arrangements, and live performances that bring those sounds to audiences.
